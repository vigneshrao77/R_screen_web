## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard is a noisy SaaS dashboard. We refuse that. This surface treats each candidate like a curated work of art, prioritizing focus, restraint, and an expansive editorial reading measure.

OWN-WORLD: Museum exhibition catalog. High contrast purely achromatic fields. Type-driven columns. Crisp, minimal hairlines instead of rounded borders or shadows. Color is restricted entirely to hairline bands indicating status. 

STORY: The recruiter is not managing a database, they are curating a collection. They select a candidate and see the parsed details presented with the elegance and clarity of a printed dossier, allowing deep reading and rapid decision-making.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left column (30%) is a tight, minimalist table of uploaded resumes. Right column (70%) is a pristine, full-height review panel with comfortable reading measures, large elegant serif headers, and strict typographic hierarchy for skills and experience.

FORM: The Exhibition Catalog (Option 1). Seed key 6bfa4de2.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
